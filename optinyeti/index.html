A popup bolted onto a bigger tool is still an afterthought.

Most CRO and analytics suites treat lead capture as a side feature. That means no exit intent, no templates built for conversion, and integrations scattered across settings pages instead of living on the campaign itself.

  • Destination and routing settings live outside the popup, not on it.
  • No exit intent, so a leaving visitor gets no last offer.
  • No attribution, so a popup conversion never gets tied to revenue.

The features that actually move conversion

Six capabilities built directly into every campaign, not buried three menus deep in a settings page.

Exit-intent technology

Detect the moment a visitor's cursor heads for the back button and trigger an offer before you lose them for good.

Revenue attribution

Follow a popup conversion downstream and see which campaigns are actually influencing pipeline.

Countdown timers

Native urgency for deadline-driven promotions, no plugin required.

Webhooks and Zapier

Route every lead straight into your CRM or automation platform. No manual exports.

Referrer and UTM targeting

Show a different offer depending on whether traffic came from an ad, an email, or search.

Campaign scheduling

Start and stop every campaign automatically, so a seasonal push ends on its own instead of living on your site for months after the promotion is over.

See what's actually converting

Every campaign reports views, conversion rate, and performance by template and trigger, so you know what to keep running and what to retire.
