From question to change in one chat

Ask how an account is pacing, which campaigns are spending without converting, or why CPA moved, and Markitech pulls the numbers directly from Google Ads. When a change is warranted, it proposes the exact edit, such as pausing a campaign or changing a daily budget, and makes it only after you approve.

Campaign plans with real structure

Describe the offer and the audience, and Markitech drafts the campaign: themed ad groups, keywords, responsive search ad copy, bidding strategy and conversion goals, based on your landing page and your account's results. You build it in Google Ads; creating campaigns directly is not available yet.

Measurement that bidding can trust

Smart bidding is only as good as the conversions it learns from. Markitech loads your pages in a real browser to check that your Google Ads conversion tags fire, and checks that GA4 receives the same events, so bidding optimizes toward real outcomes.

FAQ

Google Ads AI Agent questions

Does it need a Google Ads developer token?

No. You connect your Google Ads account through Google's sign-in, and Markitech handles the API access.

Can it create campaigns?

Not yet. Markitech drafts the full campaign plan, including ad groups, keywords and ad copy, for you to build in Google Ads. It can pause or enable campaigns and change daily budgets, each after your approval.

Can it read search terms?

Not directly yet. Upload a search terms export and Markitech groups the waste and drafts a negative keyword list. Its live wasted-spend check flags campaigns that spend without converting.

Does it support Enhanced CPC?

Google retired Enhanced CPC for Search and Display campaigns in 2025. Markitech recommends current bidding strategies such as Maximize Conversions, Target CPA and Target ROAS.
