Markitech vs Markifact

Both connect AI to your marketing platforms and ask before changing anything. They differ in breadth, in how you work with them, and in how deep they go on tracking.

Updated November 4, 2026 · Markitech team

The short answer

Choose Markifact if you want the widest coverage, with more than 50 tools including Shopify, HubSpot, Klaviyo, Snapchat Ads and Google Workspace, plus visual workflows and agents that run on a schedule.

Choose Markitech if your work centers on Google Ads, GA4 and Tag Manager and tracking is where your problems start: it has 48 Tag Manager tools for web and server containers, a real-browser tracking test, and approvals enforced by the system on every change.

Where Markifact is the better fit

  • You need LinkedIn, Microsoft or TikTok Ads connected today, or commerce, CRM and email tools such as Shopify, HubSpot or Klaviyo.
  • You want recurring reports, alerts and audits built as visual workflows, or started from templates.
  • You want agents that run on a schedule, or you want to work from Slack or WhatsApp.
  • You want to start with trial credits before choosing a plan.

Where Markitech is the better fit

  • Your day-to-day work is Google Ads, GA4 and Tag Manager, with Meta, Search Console, Merchant Center and Google Workspace alongside.
  • Tracking is where your problems start: Tag Manager audits and publishing, server containers, Consent Mode and Conversions API testing, checked in a real browser.
  • You want answers inside the AI assistant your team already uses, through a read-only MCP connection, and changes made in one app with an approval on each.
  • You want a fixed monthly credit allowance, shared across a Team workspace, with credit packs if you need more.

Pricing

List prices are the same for the entry plans: $79 a month for Pro and $249 a month to start on Team, with two months free on annual billing for both. The difference is what each plan includes. Markitech Pro includes 5,000 credits a month and Team includes 20,000 shared credits, with packs of 1,000, 5,000 or 20,000 credits for $19, $79 or $249. Markifact sells Team in tiers by seats and credits. See the Markitech plans and Markifact’s site for current details.

How to decide

Which platforms must be connected?

If you need LinkedIn, Microsoft or TikTok Ads, or commerce, CRM and email tools, Markifact covers more of them today. If your stack is Google plus Meta and you need depth in tracking, Markitech is built for that.

Do you want automations or an assistant?

Markifact leans toward workflows that run on their own. Markitech leans toward an assistant you direct, with every change reviewed before it runs.

Where does your team already work?

Markitech works in its own app and answers questions inside AI assistants that support MCP. Markifact offers its own workspace as well as MCP, Slack and WhatsApp.

Questions

Is Markitech the same company as Markifact?

No. Markitech is an independent product and is not affiliated with Markifact.

Do Markitech and Markifact both ask before changing accounts?

Yes. Markitech holds every write action until a person approves it, enforced by the system. Markifact says changes require human approval before they go live.

Which one costs less?

The entry list prices match: $79 a month for Pro and $249 a month to start on Team. Compare what each plan includes, such as seats and credits, against how your team will use it.

Can I use Markitech with the AI assistant I already have?

Yes, for questions. Add Markitech's MCP server to an assistant that supports remote MCP servers, such as Claude, and sign in. MCP access is read-only today; changes are made and approved in the Markitech app.
