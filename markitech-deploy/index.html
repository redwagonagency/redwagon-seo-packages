FAQ

Questions people ask

Will Markitech make changes to my accounts without asking?

No. Every write action — pausing a campaign, changing a budget, publishing a tag — is proposed in chat and held until you approve it. This is enforced by the system, not just by the AI following instructions, so it holds even if you tell it to skip confirmation.

What can Markitech read without asking?

Reporting and lookups — campaign lists, performance metrics, GA4 reports, and similar read-only data — run automatically so you don’t have to approve every question you ask.

How are my credentials stored?

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and never leave Markitech’s servers. Markitech connects to your accounts through each platform’s own authorization flow — it never sees or stores your platform password.

Which platforms are supported right now?

Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Search Console, Merchant Center, Business Profile, Display & Video 360, BigQuery, Google Sheets, Docs and Slides, Meta Ads, Facebook Pages, Instagram, WhatsApp Business and WordPress — 16 platforms today. LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Advertising and TikTok Ads are coming soon. See all integrations.

Is this affiliated with any other marketing AI product?

No. Markitech is an independent product, built from scratch, not affiliated with or reselling any other company’s service.
