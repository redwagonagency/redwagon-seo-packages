Works across every platform

Site tracking test

Loads any page in a real, isolated browser and reports which analytics and ad requests actually fire: GA4 events with consent state, Tag Manager containers, Google Ads, Floodlight, Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn.

Specialist investigations

Runs several focused, read-only investigations in parallel, for example tracking, paid media and analytics, and returns findings with evidence.

Your own playbooks

Searches your knowledge library of SOPs, examples, policies and brand material, so recommendations follow how your team works.

Web page reader

Reads any public page, such as a landing page or a competitor's offer, to ground ad copy and audits in what is actually on the page.

File analysis

Upload exports such as a search terms report or an Ads Manager report and Markitech analyzes them with your live data.

Approved by you, every time

Reporting and lookups run freely on every connected platform. A change is proposed with the exact account and values and held until a person approves it.
